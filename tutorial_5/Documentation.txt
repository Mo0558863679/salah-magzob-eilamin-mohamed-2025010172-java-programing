2.1. Why are the variables declared as private?

The variables are declared as private to protect the student's information from being accessed or changed directly from outside the Student class. This is an example of encapsulation.

2.2. What happens if the variables are declared public?

If the variables are declared public, other classes can directly access and modify them. This can make the data less secure and can lead to unwanted or invalid changes.

2.3. Why do we use getters and setters?

Getters are used to safely access the values of private variables, while setters are used to modify their values. They provide controlled access to the data.
